Who Am I?

A little context
before you scroll.

Born and brought up in Mumbai, with roots in Darbhanga, Bihar. Professionally, I work in HR. Personally, I collect interests like tabs in a browser.

So, what am I actually?

HR by profession. Curious by default. I like people, conversations, stories and creating things. Sometimes I write. Sometimes I click. Sometimes I paint. Sometimes I write a rap that should probably never leave my Notes app.

And these days, I like playing with AI — because apparently my existing hobbies weren't enough.

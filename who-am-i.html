WHO AM I?

So… who is
Praveen?

I'm an HR professional by profession, but that's probably the easiest answer. I'm someone who is constantly curious about people, stories, ideas and the wonderfully unpredictable things life throws at us.

The people who made me, me.

Born and brought up in Mumbai, with my roots in Darbhanga, Bihar, I've always had a little bit of both worlds around me. The fast pace of Mumbai. And the connection to where my family comes from.

I grew up in a family of four — my father, my mother, my sister and me.

My sister is now in college, slowly starting to look at the world with her own eyes. Which is slightly strange... because I still remember when she was the little sister. Apparently, time doesn't take our permission before moving on.

The Mumbai chapter.

My education happened entirely in Mumbai. And let's just say... I attended college. I also occasionally attended places that were definitely not college.

I did my share of bunking, figuring things out, changing plans, making questionable decisions and somehow still making it through.

Looking back, education taught me things. But life outside the classroom probably taught me a little more. People, conversations, mistakes, experiences and simply figuring out what I wanted to do.

The professional me.

My career didn't start with a perfectly designed five-year plan. I started somewhere completely different, moved into recruitment, took a break, came back, and eventually found myself building a career in HR and Talent Acquisition.

Along the way I've learned about hiring, people, communication, responsibility, leadership and the wonderfully complicated world of workplaces.

But the biggest thing I've learned? People are rarely as simple as their CVs make them look.

And that's probably one of the reasons I enjoy HR.

And then there's the other me.

I write. Not because I'm a writer.

I click pictures. Not because I'm a photographer.

I write raps. I paint sometimes. I experiment with AI. And I have thoughts at extremely inconvenient hours.

I've also completed my Master's in Overthinking.

IST — International School of Thinking.

No certificate.
No attendance requirement.
Unfortunately, the course is still ongoing.

Professional wrestling.

Not because I love violence. I love storytelling.

The entrances. The characters. The rivalries. The betrayals. The comebacks. The ridiculous moments. And those moments that somehow make you care about two people pretending to fight in a ring.

Whether it's WWE, AEW, AAA or anywhere else, what interests me is the storytelling behind it.

Because when it's done right, professional wrestling isn't just about what happens inside the ring. It's about making you care about why it happens.

And I think that's what I like about stories in general.

So… who am I really?

Probably someone still figuring that out.

I'm an HR professional. A son. A brother. A guy from Mumbai with roots in Darbhanga. Someone who likes stories, photographs, ideas, wrestling, music, writing and occasionally disappearing into his own thoughts.

Some things about me are professional. Some are creative. Some are completely random. And some probably make sense only inside my head.

That's why this website exists.

Not to show you just what I do.
But to show you the different things that make me, me.


You can know my designation in five seconds.
Knowing me might take a little longer.

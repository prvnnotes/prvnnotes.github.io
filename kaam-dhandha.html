BEFORE THE TITLE
I didn't start
knowing where
I'd end up.

My professional journey started somewhere completely different from where I am today.


Working on interior projects meant being around people, managing work on the ground, dealing with vendors, handling timelines and solving problems when things didn't go according to plan.


Years later, the environment changed. The people didn't. Managing people, understanding situations, solving problems and keeping things moving remained strangely familiar.

WHAT I'VE LEARNED SO FAR

A job gives you a title.
Experience gives you a perspective.

I'm still figuring things out. Probably always will. But every role, every person and every slightly unexpected turn has added something to the person doing the work today.
